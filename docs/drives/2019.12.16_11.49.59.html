The drive, second by second

Line colour is the serving technology. Point at the chart to read all three values at one moment. Dots mark serving-cell changes; a black dot starts a ping-pong round trip (A to B and back within 5 s).

Coverage and throughput

RSRP classes show how much of the drive had weak, fair, good or excellent signal. Throughput is measured while the phone was downloading.

Cell changes

Each change interrupts the data flow for a moment. Changes inside a ping-pong round trip are the wasteful ones: the phone ends up where it started.

Map

The route in 50 m road bins. Switch layers in the top right corner: RSRP, throughput, technology, cell changes, ping-pong round trips. A ring marks a bin with poor RSRP.

Data: D. Raca, D. Leahy, C. J. Sreenan and J. J. Quinlan, "Beyond Throughput, The Next Generation: a 5G Dataset with Channel and Context Metrics", ACM MMSys 2020 (uccmisl/5Gdataset, GPL-3.0). Download-while-driving logs from Cork, Ireland, recorded with G-NetTrack Pro.

Every number on this page is computed from the raw logs by this code.

The log is application-level: it shows serving-cell changes, not the handover signalling. A change can be a handover, a reselection or a re-establishment.

On 5G rows the radio values most likely describe the LTE anchor cell (5G NSA). That is an inference from the data; the log does not state it.
